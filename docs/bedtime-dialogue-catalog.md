# Bedtime Dialogue Catalog

The initial bedtime catalog contains at least 30 authored Japanese lines split across three moments:

- ARRIVAL: entering the bedtime scene
- SETTLING: the companion is getting comfortable
- LIGHTS_OUT: the final quiet moment before sleep

Relationship-specific lines are gated by minimum familiarity. Later stages can still use neutral lines, but when both are eligible the selector prefers the most relationship-specific content.

LIGHTS_OUT lines are intentionally short. The catalog avoids medical advice, sleep-quality judgement, productivity coaching, guilt, streak language, and excited punctuation.

The selector applies the same recent-ID cooldown used by the broader dialogue system, so repeated nights can rotate through eligible lines without requiring generated text.
